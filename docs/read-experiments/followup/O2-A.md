# O2-A / O2-B — admitted plaintext ownership and bounded physical ranges

The two O2-A counterexamples were executed against `1f4cab1` with only the test fixture and a task-scoped allocation observer added. The observer wraps the existing `Vec<u8>` in a `Bytes` owner, charges its actual `capacity()`, and releases that charge in the owner's destructor. It does not change the baseline selection or capacity policy. The failed binary, patch, fixture and logs are retained in the local follow-up evidence directory.

| Executed path | Returned payload | Before: unique backing capacity | Corrected capacity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Frozen two-segment scan | 2 bytes | 14,680,066 bytes | 2 bytes |
| Two simultaneous frozen scan results | 4 bytes | 29,360,132 bytes | 4 bytes |
| Clipped fork, unpolled binary response body | 1 byte | 7,340,033 bytes | 1 byte |

The scan fixture creates a collection, executes a real split, appends one byte to each live segment, freezes both exclusive frontiers at one, then appends fourteen 512 KiB records per segment through the real committer/encryption/WAL path. Compression and the tail ring are disabled. The fork fixture creates the parent and child through HTTP and appends the suffix after the fork boundary. Both tests assert exact payloads and cursors as well as backing capacity, and require the charge to return to zero after the final result/body drop. The peer control sends the bounded range through the real internal HTTP endpoint and asserts one returned record, consumed position one, and the truthful physical frontier of fifteen.

`ReadRange` now carries the physical exclusive end through local execution, frozen collection scans, sealed replay spans, fork ancestors, partial-record fork creation, SSE lineage and peer requests. The executor caps storage scans before materialization while preserving actual durable/applied frontiers in the result. Match-free progress and partial-page semantics remain separate from emitted record count.

`PlainBatch` replaces the movable `Vec<PlainRec>` and optional contiguous hint. Its private blocks own exact-capacity immutable storage and authenticated record ranges. Record payloads expose read-only bytes; they cannot clone or transfer a shared backing owner independently. One central `append_selected` operation admits selected bytes against the caller's existing page budget. It transfers complete blocks without copying payloads or rebuilding their record vectors and compacts incomplete blocks before retaining them. The private block index is constructed monotonically with record admission and adjusted on transfers; record lookup does not walk every preceding record.

Publishing a block converts the admitted buffer into an exact-length boxed allocation. A complete pre-sized uncompressed buffer transfers directly. Truncated prefixes and fallback buffers may shrink once. Consequently, the sum of admitted payload bytes is also the sum of unique plaintext backing capacities, including owners retained by binary bodies. `contiguous()` can return a body owner only when the batch contains exactly one complete block. The last `Bytes` owner controls storage release; the regression observer follows that same lifetime rather than the producing function or batch wrapper.

Consumer delivery retains an admitted batch with an offset index while its queue transaction runs. Partial-record fork materialization copies only the chosen prefix. These changes avoid reintroducing shared-subset ownership through the former per-record clone paths. SSE's existing cursor tests were moved unchanged into a companion test module to keep the production source within its existing file budget.

For O2-B, `Decrypted` distinguishes an appended range from independently decoded storage. The bounded authenticated fallback transfers its buffer directly into the same batch abstraction; it no longer copies each decompressed record into an aggregate or repeatedly calls `reserve_exact` on that aggregate. Uncompressed runs after a fallback use amortized `Vec` growth and shrink once at publication. Encryption formats, nonce/AAD inputs, mixed legacy/current decoding, decompression limits and first-record/page limits are unchanged. These are structural bounds, not an allocation-free or service-performance claim; the allocator measurements are reported separately in the follow-up experiment report.

Validation includes seven focused O2 tests: real scan/fork lifetimes, subset compaction, full-owner identity retention, outer-budget withholding, mixed compressed authentication failure and withholding, and pre-sized in-place decrypt reuse. Broader executed controls include 4 application-read, 3 page/peer, 6 ring, 3 applied-read, 10 fork, 12 crypto, 1 cipher-cache, 11 postings, 5 R17-B retirement and 32 SSE tests. These groups overlap the focused set; they are not an additive count of unique tests. All-target Clippy and the inventory, architecture, scenario and review-source gates are checked without changing their historical baselines. The full final-source gate and performance decision remain part of the final follow-up package.
