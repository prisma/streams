## Executive summary

**New services are not being published to the edge in any of the six
regions we use** — with one measured exception: a ~3-minute window
around 07:11, during which six freshly-created services (one per
region) all received routes within a minute and still serve today.
Everything created at ≥07:16 — twenty-six services across ten projects,
four measurement rounds, two patient probes and two canaries — has
never served a byte through its domain, while:

- the control plane reports every version `running`;
- the machines boot normally (platform logs show source unpack, and the
  app's own `aighty booting` line — captured today for all six final
  services);
- pre-existing services answer 200 through the same edge throughout;
- the 404 is the platform's canned "There is no service on this URL"
  HTML, returned in ~0.1–0.3 s (edge-fast, not a timeout).

The working window is the sharpest lead we can offer: **whatever
programs edge routes was working at 07:11–07:13 and not at ≥07:16, and
had also not been working on 2026-07-31** (previous retry, same
symptom, 16-minute patient poll). It looks like an intermittently
healthy publication pipeline, not a per-region or per-app fault.

## CORRECTION (same day, ~10:30 UTC): most of today's failures are a PORT-MAPPING default, not route publication

Piero/Alexey's conduit hint (`portMapping {"http":3000}` on a failing
service) led to a three-arm experiment in `proj_triqoh1fff5170jnpr1ik40b`
(us-east-1, identical 15-line app, same CLI 0.38.0):

| arm | flags | app port | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cpv_n98yrodrwx9cs73ztb0bhvkh` | `--http-port 8080` | 8080 | **200** |
| `cpv_g5na3rbzpmifbwjo3q4quebs` | *(none)* | 8080 | **404** (canned page) |
| `cpv_qv3cd0dk05dze981jtym81jg` | *(none)* | 3000 | **200** |

So: a flag-less deploy is mapped to **3000** (the CLI help says
`default: 8080`), and a port-mismatched backend renders as the same
"There is no service on this URL" page as a missing route — the two
failure modes are indistinguishable from outside, which is how today's
campaign mis-attributed them. Every failing deploy in this report was
flag-less; the working 07:11-window set was ALSO flag-less, which is
the remaining puzzle for your side:

- **Please check conduit portMapping for the working set**
  (`proj_hhnx51y6schuevg7vpt35kwr`, e.g. sin `cpv_sl648utxwrw280n5luln47hm`).
  If it shows 8080, the implicit default flipped 8080→3000 at ~07:14
  UTC today, which fully explains the "3-minute window" and the "flap".
- The 2026-07-31 evidence is NOT retracted: that day's fresh
  streams-slate deployment used an explicit `--http-port 8080` and
  still 404'd through a 16-minute poll — so the original edge-
  publication incident was real and distinct from this port default.
- Round-4 specimens stay up: their `{"http":3000}` mappings are now the
  evidence for THIS finding rather than the routing one.

Repro-kit updated: `repro.sh` now passes `--http-port 8080` so the
